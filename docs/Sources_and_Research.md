## What we deliberately do not infer

Downloads are not daily active users. Daily active users are not concurrent calls. Customer coins and listener diamonds/roses are not necessarily convertible at the same rate. Venture funding is not revenue; revenue is not profit. A ₹1 introductory purchase is an acquisition subsidy, not evidence that a complete service can be profitably priced at ₹1. A “verified” badge must describe a check actually performed, not promise an absence of fake profiles.
